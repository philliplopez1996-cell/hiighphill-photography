About

Hello

Hello, I'm Felipe.

My path here wasn't traditional — no photography classes, just a camera, a drone, my iPhone, and a lot of curiosity. Growing up, I learned to make the most of what I had, and that mindset shapes everything I create today.

Every photo is also a reminder to slow down — to be grateful for the moment I'm in, especially when I'm around people I love.

My goal is simple: use my photography to give back, whether that's supporting a cause, a shelter, or just making someone's day a little brighter.
